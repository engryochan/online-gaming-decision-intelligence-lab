Summaries
 
derived
 
from
 
the
 
synoptic
 
and
 
hourly
 
reports
 
provided
 
in
 
the
 
Global
 
Historical
 
Climatology
 
Network
 
hourly
 
(GHCNh)
 
dataset.
  
SSOD
 
v2
 
is
 
the
 
successor
 
to
 
the
 
Global
 
Summary
 
of
 
the
 
Day
 
(GSOD)
 
dataset,
 
which
 
was
 
derived
 
from
 
the
 
Integrated
 
Surface
 
Dataset
 
or
 
ISD.
  
GHCNh
 
is
 
the
 
successor
 
dataset
 
to
 
ISD.
  
  The  SSOD  daily  summaries  are  based  on  hourly  (e.g.  METAR/FM15)  or  6-hourly  
(SYNOP/FM16)
 
reports
 
for
 
a
 
calendar
 
day
 
that
 
runs
 
from
 
00
 
to
 
24
 
UTC.
 
In
 
this
 
way,
 
all
 
daily
 
climate
 
summaries
 
in
 
SSOD
 
nominally
 
conform
 
to
 
the
 
same
 
synchronous
 
24-hour
 
period
 
(i.e,
 
the
 
same
 
“synoptic
 
day")
 
worldwide.
 
This
 
contrasts
 
with
 
the
 
daily
 
summaries
 
provided
 
in
 
Global
 Historical  Climatology  Network  daily  (GHCNd)  dataset.  In  GHCNd,  daily  climate  summaries  are  provided  by  national  Met  Services  and  are  compiled  according  to  the  observation  time  and  
conventions
 
used
 
in
 
their
 
country
 
for
 
daily
 
climate
 
reports
 
(e.g.,
 
midnight
 
local
 
time
 
for
 
US
 
Airports,
 
06
 
UTC
 
stations
 
in
 
Canada,
 
9
 
am
 
local
 
in
 
Australia
 
etc.).
   
  The  utility  of  SSOD  is  that  daily  summaries  are  provided  in  short  time  delay  for  the  network  of  
many
 
thousands
 
of
 
stations
 
that
 
transmit
 
hourly
 
and
 
synoptic
 
reports.
 
This
 
ensures
 
the
 
SSOD
 
summary  day  is  consistent  for  all  stations  worldwide,  in  theory  making  comparisons  to  numerical  
weather
 
prediction
 
model
 
output
 
and
 
remotely
 
sensed
 
data
 
more
 
straightforward.
 
One
 
disadvantage
 
of
 
SSOD
 
is
 
that
 
daily
 
precipitation
 
totals
 
may
 
be
 
underreported
 
in
 
the
 
synoptic
 
messages.
 
Additionally,
 
maximum
 
and
 
minimum
 
temperatures
 
gleaned
 
from
 
the
 
hourly
 
or
 
synoptic
 
messages
 
also
 
may
 
not
 
correspond
 
to
 
the
 
true
 
daily
 
extremes.
 
The
 
daily
 
summaries
 
provided
 
by
 
national
 
Met
 
Services
 
in
 
GHCNd
 
are
 
therefore
 
recommended
 
when
 
the
 
most
 
accurate
 
reports
 
are
 
required
 
for
 
daily
 
rainfall
 
and
 
maximum/minimum
 
temperatures.
 
However,
 
as
 
noted
 
above,
 
the
 
24-hour
 
periods
 
represented
 
in
 
the
 
daily
 
summaries
 
provided
 
in
 
GHCNd
 
are
 
not
 
the
 
same
 
worldwide.
  
This
 
distinction
 
between
 
the
 
consistent
 
summary
 
day
 
in
 
SSOD
 
and
 
the
 
mix
 
of
 
summary
 
days
 
in
 
GHCNd
 
is
 
the
 
reason
 
for
 
the
 
name
 
change
 
from
 
Global
 
Summary
 
of
 
the
 
Day
 
(GSOD)
 
to
 
Synoptic
 
Summary
 
of
 
the
 
Day
 
(SSOD,
 
version
 
2).
     
  The  headers  used  in  csv  files  and  definition  of  each  daily  element  included  in  the  dataset  (as   available  from  each  station)  are  as  follows:   Column  Number    1  STATION  
GHCN  identifier  
2  Station_name  
Station  Name  from  source  metadata  
3  DATE  
 Date  (YYYY-MM-DD)  in  UTC   
4  Year  
Year  of  Daily  Summary  (in  UTC)  
5  Month  
Month  of  Daily  Summary  (in  UTC)  
6  Day  
Day  of  Daily  Summary  (in  UTC)  
7  LATITUDE  
Station  Latitude  (decimal  degrees)  
8  LONGITUDE  
Station  Longitude  (decimal  degrees)  9  ELEVATION  Elevation  (meters)  
10  mean_temperature  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off  synoptic  hourly  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)--in  °C  
11  mean_temperature_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24).  For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
12  mean_dew_point_temperature  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off-  synoptic  hourly  dew  point  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)  in  degrees  C.   For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
13  mean_dew_point_temperature_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24)  
14  mean_sea_level_pressure  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off  synoptic  hourly  dew  point  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)--in  hPa.   For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
15  mean_sea_level_pressure_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24)  
16  mean_station_level_pressure  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off  synoptic  hourly  dew  point  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)--in  hPa.   For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
17  mean_station_level_pressure_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24)  
18  mean_visibility  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off  synoptic  hourly  dew  point  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)--in  km.   For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
19  mean_visibility_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24)  
20  mean_wind_speed  
Average  of  synoptic  hourly  or  syntopic  +  off  synoptic  hourly  dew  point  temperatures  (from  00  to  24  UTC)--in  
meters  per  second.   For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
21  mean_wind_speed_Measurement_Code  
 Number  of  hourly  values  available  (from  4  to  24)  
22  max_wind_speed  
Maximum  wind  speed  reported  for  the  day–in  meters  per  second  
23  max_wind_gust  
 Maximum  wind  gust  reported  for  the  day–in  meters  per  second  
24  max_temperature  
Maximum  temperature  for  the  day  drive  from  hourly  reports  or  from  the  climate  code  group  in  the  SYNOP/FM12  report–in  °C  
25  max_temperature_Measurement_Code  
*  indicates  value  was  derived  from  the  hourly  data  (FM15/METAR/SA/  etc)  rather  than  from  the  climate  code  group  of  the  SYNOP/FM12    
26  min_temperature  
Minimum  temperature  for  the  day  drive  from  hourly  reports  or  from  the  climate  code  group  in  the  SYNOP/FM12  report–For  a  daily  mean  to  be  computed,  at  least  one  report  in  each  of  the  6-hour  synoptic  periods  (00:00  to  05:59  UTC,  
06:00  to  11:59  UTC,  12:00  to  17:59  UTC,  and  18:00  to  23:59  UTC)  must  be  available.  
27  min_temperature_Measurement_Code  
*  indicates  value  was  derived  from  the  hourly  data  (METAR/SA/FM15  etc)  rather  than  from  the  climate  code  group  of  the  SYNOP/FM12   
28  total_precipitation  
Total  precipitation  reported  for  the  day  as  reported  in  e.g.,  FM15/METAR  hourlies  or  SYNOP/FM12  reports  –in  mm  
29  total_precipitation_Measurement_Code  
n  (Number  of  hours)  =  Sum  of  hourly  reports,  where  n  is  the  count  of  hourly  totals  reported  A  =  1  report  of  6-hour  precipitation  amount.  B  =  Summation  of  2  reports  of  6-hour  precipitation  amount.  C  =  Summation  of  3  reports  of  6-hour  precipitation  amount.  D  =  Summation  of  4  reports  of  6-hour  precipitation  amount.  E  =  1  report  of  12-hour  precipitation  amount.  F  =  Summation  of  2  reports  of  12-hour  precipitation  amount.  G  =  1  report  of  24-hour  precipitation  amount.  H  =  Station  reported  '0'  as  the  amount  for  the  day  (eg,  from  hourly,  6-hourly  reports,  etc.)  or  the  amount  could  not  be  
calculated,  but  at  least  one  occurrence  of  precipitation  was  noted  in  the  present  weather  observations.  This  could  indicate  that  a  trace  occurred  (or  more  if  the  value  is  missing).  I  =  Station  did  not  report  any  precipitation  data  for  the  day  and  did  not  report  any  occurrences  of  precipitation  in  its  present  weather  observations.   
30  snow_depth  
From  the  last  report  for  the  day  if  reported  more  than  once–in  mm  
31  observed_pres_wx  
Observed  weather  reported  across  all  available  reports  for  the  day  for  weather  types  “Fog/Rain/Snow  or  Ice  Pellets/Hail/Thunder/Tornado  or  Funnel  Cloud”   The  presence  or  absence  of  the  above  weather  types  is  noted  as  a  zero  (‘0’)  or  a  one  (‘1’):   reported=1;  no  report=0  yielding  a  string  of  six  digits  (one  digit  for  each  weather  type).     ********************************************************************
